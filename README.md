# Corrupted Omens

A top-down bullet-heaven roguelike set in the corrupted lands of Arenthal. Fight through three levels of corruption — the farmlands, the courtyard, and the throne room — to defeat the Ancient Evil that has taken hold of the castle and save the kingdom.

## How to Launch

**Windows:** 
1. Download and extract the ZIP file
2. Run `CorruptedOmens.exe`

**Mac:**
1. Download and extract the ZIP file
2. Open `CorruptedOmens.app`
3. If blocked by Gatekeeper: right click the app → Open → Open anyway

## Controls
- **WASD** — Move
- **Space** — Activate Ultimate ability
- **Mouse cursor** — Aim Blizzard placement
- **ESC** — Pause

## Gameplay
- Survive waves of corrupted enemies across three levels
- Collect coins to permanently upgrade your wizard between runs
- Build up your ultimate charge by defeating enemies
- Fight through the farm, courtyard and throne room to reach the Mad King
- Defeat the Mad King's corrupted minions to bring him down
